# SimpleWorship's name and logo

SimpleWorship's code is free software under the
[GNU General Public License v3.0 or later](LICENSE): anyone may use it,
study it, change it and share it. The licence covers the code, not the
name.

The name **SimpleWorship** and the SimpleWorship logo are trademarks of
Prince Takyi Akomea. They tell churches that what they are installing is
the real SimpleWorship, which is always free. This page says how they may
be used.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name to talk about SimpleWorship: in reviews, tutorials,
  articles, sermons, videos and training.
- Share unchanged copies of the official releases under the name, for
  example on a USB stick for another church.
- Say that something works with SimpleWorship ("a theme pack for
  SimpleWorship"), as long as it is clear it is not from the project.
- Use the logo to link to the official website or downloads.
- Charge for your own services around it, such as installing it or
  training a church's team, if you say plainly that the software itself is
  free.

## You may not, without permission

- Share a changed version under the name SimpleWorship or with its logo.
  Give your version its own name and logo; you may say it is "based on
  SimpleWorship".
- Sell SimpleWorship, or charge for downloading it, under its name.
- Use the name or logo as part of your own product's, company's or
  website's name, or in a way that suggests the project endorses you.

## The official SimpleWorship

The official website is <https://brvhprince.github.io/simpleworship/>,
and the official downloads are the
[releases on GitHub](https://github.com/brvhprince/simpleworship/releases).
They are always free.

To ask about anything not covered here, or to report a misuse of the name,
[open an issue](https://github.com/brvhprince/simpleworship/issues/new).
