Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

Summary: SimpleWorship is offline-first, open-source software. We do not collect, track, store, or transmit your personal data. All audio transcription and presentation media remain strictly on your local computer.

1. Overview

SimpleWorship (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is developed by Prince Takyi Akomea as free, open-source software for churches. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to protecting your privacy when using the SimpleWorship desktop application and website.

2. Data Collection and Processing

SimpleWorship does not collect, sell, rent, or monetize personal information. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide your name, email address, or payment details.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: The application does not contain tracking scripts, usage telemetry, or third-party advertising SDKs.
  • Local Storage: All church presentations, songs, scripture selections, schedules, themes, and configuration files are stored locally on your device.

3. Device Capabilities and Hardware Access

To provide church presentation features, SimpleWorship may request access to specific hardware permissions:

  • Microphone (Listen Feature): When you explicitly turn on the "Listen" feature, SimpleWorship accesses your audio input to recognize spoken scripture passages. Audio is transcribed 100% locally on your computer using embedded Whisper AI models. Your audio is never recorded, never sent over the internet, and never shared with any cloud service.
  • Camera and Video Feeds: SimpleWorship accesses connected webcams or video capture cards only when you view or display them under Media › Cameras. Camera feeds are processed locally for live projection and are never transmitted to external cloud servers.
  • Local Network (Phone Remote): The phone remote feature operates exclusively over your local Wi-Fi / Local Area Network (LAN) via a local web server (port 7270). No data leaves your local network.

4. External Network Connections

The desktop application makes internet connections only for the following user-facing functions:

  • Software Updates: The app periodically queries the public GitHub Releases API to verify if a newer version of SimpleWorship is available. No user data is sent during update checks.
  • Speech Model Downloads: When you choose to download speech recognition models, the file is retrieved directly from public release repositories.
  • Bible Translations: Public-domain USFM Bible translations are downloaded directly upon user request.

5. Children's Privacy

SimpleWorship does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Open Source Transparency

SimpleWorship is free software licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 or later. Its complete source code is publicly accessible on GitHub at github.com/brvhprince/simpleworship, allowing anyone to inspect and audit our security and privacy practices.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach us by creating an issue on our GitHub Issues page or by contacting Prince Takyi Akomea.
