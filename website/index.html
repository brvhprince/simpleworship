    Bring your EasyWorship
    library with you.

    Years of songs and Bibles come across in a minute. EasyWorship's files are only read, never changed, so you can keep both while you decide.

    1. 1

      Install and open

      On the computer that runs EasyWorship, or copy its folders across on a USB stick.

    2. 2

      It finds your library

      Songs, Bibles (yes, your Twi .ewb too), backgrounds and media.

    3. 3

      Import

      Check the list and press Import. Sunday's schedule is ready to build.

    Download SimpleWorship

    Free for Mac, Windows and Linux.

    macOS

    Apple chip and Intel · macOS 11 or later

    Download for Mac
    Opening it the first time

    Open the downloaded file and drag SimpleWorship to Applications. The first time, right-click the app and choose Open, then Open again. After that it opens normally. (Apple charges a yearly fee to skip this step, which a free app can't carry.)

    Windows

    Windows 10 and 11 · 64-bit

    Download for Windows or the .msi, for IT installs
    If Windows warns you

    Windows may say it “protected your PC” because the installer isn't signed with a paid certificate. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

    Linux

    Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora and others

    Download AppImage

    or the .deb (Ubuntu, Debian) or .rpm (Fedora)

    It updates itself: new versions download quietly and install only when you say, never during a service.
